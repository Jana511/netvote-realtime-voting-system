How it works

Four steps from question to result.

Log in

Enter a username. The administrator also enters the admin password.

Create a poll

The administrator types a question and 2–10 options and starts the poll.

Vote

Everyone picks one option and submits. Results update for all users at once.

Close & save

The administrator closes the poll. The final result is saved in the history.

Ready to vote?

Log in with any username to take part in the current poll.
